## Stable versus incidental signals

High-confidence stable signals:

- an everyday or viral premise appears before the technical explanation;
- the investigation is disproportionately rigorous relative to the social importance of the question;
- definitions, variables, measurements, or calculations provide real explanatory value;
- colloquial reaction and formal reasoning alternate;
- the ending often connects the result to curiosity, problem framing, or human judgment.

Lower-confidence or incidental signals:

- exact punctuation, exclamation density, and headline length;
- recurring personal characters and office anecdotes;
- platform-specific image placement;
- any distinctive catchphrase or autobiographical detail.
