# Source Notes

Evidence reviewed on 2026-10-25. These notes identify repeatable mechanisms; they do not preserve full copyrighted article text.

## Primary sample set

### 1. AI挖出藏了十几年的底层PHP漏洞，网络安全也回不去那个旧时代了

- Date: 2026-10-22
- Mirror: https://www.panewslab.com/zh/articles/01a1618c-d7e0-71b8-aa1f-f7a0a8692345
- Role in sample: technical incident and industry consequence.
- Observed mechanism: opens with a concrete vulnerability, reconstructs why older auditing missed it, then explains how AI changes discovery cost rather than merely announcing that AI found a bug.
- Stable signal: old problem → new capability → changed economics.

### 2. 有人做了35万个AI，只为了反过来骗骗子

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Mirror: https://m.pedaily.cn/news/570495
- Role in sample: social story and value-oriented ending.
- Observed mechanism: an absurd but specific number creates the hook; founder scene and scam economics turn novelty into mechanism; a related Chinese case broadens the story before the ending returns to human choice.
- Stable signal: surprising event → person → mechanism → second case → restrained value judgment.

### 3. 实测Gemini 4 Argon - Google这跌跌撞撞的背水一战

- Date: 2026-10-14
- Mirror: https://m.pedaily.cn/news/570221
- Role in sample: paid product test.
- Observed mechanism: provides launch context quickly, discloses the writer's paid access and preference, shows tests and screenshots, then converts results into a recommendation rather than repeating the feature list.
- Stable signal: personal cost and visible operation support an early judgment.

### 4. AI开始逆向一切，你电脑里的每个软件都要被迫开源了

- Date: 2026-10-12
- Mirror: https://www.uisdc.com/ai-reverse-everything
- Role in sample: technical explainer scaled into a business-model argument.
- Observed mechanism: explains reverse engineering from first principles, uses historical and personal examples, then shows how easier reproduction may push software toward thinner clients and cloud control.
- Stable signal: mechanism → history → firsthand example → commercial consequence → unresolved ownership question.

## Cross-sample quantitative reference

An independent analysis of 131 articles published between February and July 2026 reports:

- 83 of 131 articles concerned tools, workflows, or model/product testing;
- 127 of 131 used a clear first-person perspective;
- visible preferences, immediate context, real actions, costs, failures, reversals, and uncertainty were more consistent indicators of “human voice” than casual wording alone.

Source: https://viyi.cc/docs/ip_khazix_social_media_analysis/01_wechat_content_distribution_analysis/

Treat these figures as a secondary analysis of that dataset, not as platform-wide performance data.

## Excluded or limited evidence

- Article directories were used to establish recency, not to infer quality from unavailable read counts.
- Reposts and mirrors may alter typography or image placement, so layout-specific claims are low confidence.
- Sponsored passages should not be used to infer native editorial voice unless the same mechanism repeats in non-sponsored articles.
- Memorable phrases and personal anecdotes are not reusable rules.

## Confidence summary

- High: first-person actions function as evidence; concrete anomalies lead; articles scale from test or mechanism to industry consequence; judgments arrive before exhaustive explanation.
- Medium: typical paragraph length, meme frequency, and exact title patterns because mirrors may change formatting and the sample is small.
- Low: traffic performance and conversion impact because comparable analytics are unavailable.
