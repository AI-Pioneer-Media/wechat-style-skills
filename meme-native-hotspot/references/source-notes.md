## Stable versus mode-specific signals

Stable across the sample:

- first-person encounter before explanation;
- multiple concrete examples in the opening;
- colloquial reactions attached to visible evidence;
- movement from spectacle to platform, product, or business mechanism;
- short mobile-friendly paragraphs and image-led pacing.

More likely mode-specific:

- product tutorials and direct calls to action;
- brand-comparison framing;
- discount or purchase information;
- unusually clean resolution of the opening problem.

Treat these as sponsored-story signals unless repeated in clearly non-sponsored work.

## Evidence limits

- Read counts are unavailable or incomparable, so “representative” means structurally useful rather than top-performing.
- Nicknames and distinctive slang are surface expression, not reusable rules.
